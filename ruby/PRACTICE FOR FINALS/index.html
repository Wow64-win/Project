About Us

Introduction

We are a group of students who made a decision to develop a website that makes checking attendance easier, faster, and more efficient. Our goal is to provide a simple and convenient solution to the common challenges of traditional attendance-taking methods. Our system allows students to generate their own unique QR codes through our website, which can then be placed on their school IDs for easy identification. Using a camera scanner, the system can read each student's QR code, identify their information, and automatically record their attendance. Instead of manually writing down names or checking students one at a time, teachers and authorized personnel can simply scan each student's ID to keep track of who is present. This helps reduce the time spent on attendance-taking while minimizing errors during manual recording. Through this project, we aim to introduce a more organized and reliable way of managing student attendance records. We also hope to demonstrate how technology can improve everyday school activities and make tasks more convenient for both students and teachers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a fast and reliable attendance monitoring system using QR codes. We aim to make the attendance process more convenient by reducing manual recording, minimizing errors and keeping student attendance records organized. Through this website, we want to use current technology to improve the way attendance is managed in our school and make the attendance process easier for both students and teachers.

Our Vision

Our goal is to create a system that is trusted and works by scanning QR codes on IDs. This system helps track student attendance and makes taking attendance faster, simpler and more organized. We want to improve the way attendance is taken by letting students use their school IDs for identification and digital attendance recording.
